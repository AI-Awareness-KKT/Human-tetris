How the Game Works

Match 15 different silhouettes, once each in a random order. Keep both feet on the floor and hold each pose steady as the wall arrives.

Copy the on-screen pose and hold it as the wall arrives. Keep both feet on the floor.

Without a camera, use keys 1–9, 0, Z, X, C, V and B or the pose buttons.

Features of the Game

  • Full-body camera controls with a visible skeleton preview.
  • Clearly labelled keyboard demo for testing without a webcam.
  • Easy, Normal and Hard challenges; pause, restart and fullscreen controls.
  • Local personal bests stored separately for camera and demo modes.
  • Camera frames processed in the browser; no recordings or uploads.

How to Play

  1. Click Open camera and allow camera access.
  2. Stand upright with your head, hands and feet visible; hold still until calibration finishes.
  3. Copy the on-screen pose and hold it as the wall arrives. Keep both feet on the floor.
  4. Click Start camera game. You can pause, restart or change difficulty between sessions.

Use a clear space and comfortable movements. No jumping or diving is required. Internet is needed to download the tracking model and runtime. The keyboard demo works without the model.
