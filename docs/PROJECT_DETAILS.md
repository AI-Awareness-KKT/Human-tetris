# Human Tetris

Match 15 different silhouettes, once each in a random order. Keep both feet on the floor and hold each pose steady as the wall arrives.

## How to play

1. Enable body tracking and allow camera access.
2. Stand upright with your head, hands and feet visible; hold still until calibration finishes.
3. Copy the target silhouette with both feet on the floor. New shapes use one arm raised or one arm raised with the other extended sideways. Left and right refer to the mirrored on-screen silhouette. Hold the match during the last moments before the wall reaches you. No jumping, single-leg balancing, spinning or floor poses are required.
4. Click Start camera game. You can pause, restart or change difficulty between sessions.

## Demo controls

Use keys 1–9 and 0 for the first ten poses, then Z, X, C, V and B for the last five. You can also select any of the 15 pose buttons below the arena.
