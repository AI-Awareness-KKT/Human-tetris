WELCOME TO THE MOTION LAB

Human Tetris

Match 15 different silhouettes, once each in a random order. Keep both feet on the floor and hold each pose steady as the wall arrives.

Camera controls

Copy the target silhouette with both feet on the floor. Follow raised-arm, low-V, diagonal and cross-body shapes. Keep both hands visible, including when bringing them near your chest. Left and right refer to the mirrored on-screen silhouette. Hold the match during the last moments before the wall reaches you. No jumping, single-leg balancing, spinning or floor poses are required. Enable tracking, stand upright with your whole body in view, and hold still for the calibration bar. Then start the game. Tracking loss pauses play; return to view and press Resume.

Keyboard demo

Use keys 1–9 and 0 for the first ten poses, then Z, X, C, V and B for the last five. You can also select any of the 15 pose buttons below the arena. Demo inputs animate a virtual body. They do not use AI or your camera.

Good to know

Play within a comfortable clear space. No jumping or diving is required. Camera mode needs Internet to load the model and HTTPS or localhost for camera permission. Scores are saved only in this browser, separately by game, mode and difficulty.
